Journey through Lucid

A grief-stricken neuroscientist descends through seven levels of a shared dreamspace to recover the last words her mother never finished saying — and discovers that consciousness does not end. It waits.

SCROLL TO DESCEND ↓
When a Lagos neuroscientist discovers her deceased mother has been leaving encoded messages inside a shared dreamspace, she must descend through seven dream levels — facing the dissolution of identity itself — before the dream collapses and her mother is lost forever.
The Architecture of Dream

Seven Levels Deep

The dreamspace obeys five laws. Emotional gravity shapes the landscape. Memory builds the architecture. Lucidity is the currency of power — and of risk.

The Dreamers

Those Who Descend

🌕
Dr. Adaeze Okonkwo
PROTAGONIST · NEUROSCIENTIST · LAGOS
Her mother died mid-sentence eight months ago. She has not slept deeply since. Configured by her ori for a destiny she has spent her career denying — to bridge the scientific and the ancestral.
Arc: Materialist → Witness → Carrier of the verse
✨
Mama Chioma
THE DREAMED MOTHER · ORI HISTORIAN
She is not a ghost. She is a residue of consciousness in the shared dreamspace — the way a vibration continues after the string is struck. She has been holding the unfinished verse open like a door.
Arc: Absence → Presence → Release
🔴
Dr. Emeka Saro
GUIDE / SHADOW · CO-CREATOR OF COGA-9
The world's foremost authority on shared-consciousness dreaming. His ori-light in the dream is fractured. He has been using the dreamspace to hold on — and taking more than he gives.
Arc: Mentor → Antagonist → Broken confessor
🌀
Yewande
THE DREAM NATIVE · ORACLE
She exists only in the dreamspace. No waking-world counterpart has ever been found. She is the lucid realm becoming aware of itself — the I AM that emerged from the accumulated dreaming of all who passed through.
Arc: Symbol → Guide → Mirror
From the Script

Level Seven — The Origin

The scene no film has attempted: two consciousnesses meeting in the place before birth. The IWAS root question answered in light.

INT. DREAMSPACE — LEVEL SEVEN / THE ORIGIN — CONTINUOUS (DREAM)

WHITE.
Pure white. No architecture. No ground beneath her feet though she stands. No horizon though space extends infinitely.
Her mother.
Chioma stands ten feet away. No ori-light above her head — she IS the light. Her whole form radiates it. She looks younger than any photograph Adaeze has seen.

CHIOMA (simply)
There you are.

ADAEZE
Here I am.

A long silence. The kind that holds everything.

CHIOMA
This is not mine to keep. It was always for you. Not because you are my daughter — because you are who your ori was shaped to be. The one who would carry it back.

ADAEZE (tears falling — she didn't know she could cry here)
Back to where?

CHIOMA
To the world. To the people who are still asleep and don't know they're dreaming.

Adaeze reaches for the verse. The moment she touches it — she understands everything. We see it on her face. Not an explanation. A recognition.
The white blazes.
